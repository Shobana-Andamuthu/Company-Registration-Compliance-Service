Preparation

Documents checklist

Keep these ready to speed up your registration.

  • PAN and Aadhaar of all directors
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof (bank statement or utility bill)
  • Registered office proof and owner NOC
  • Latest electricity or water bill of the office
  • PAN and Aadhaar of all partners
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof of partners
  • Registered office proof and owner NOC
  • Partnership contribution details
  • PAN of the business or proprietor
  • Aadhaar and photograph of the owner
  • Business address proof and rent agreement
  • Bank account details (cancelled cheque)
  • Business incorporation certificate, if applicable
