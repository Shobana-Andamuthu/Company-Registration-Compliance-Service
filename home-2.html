FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what founders ask us most.

How long does company registration take?

A Private Limited company is typically incorporated in 7 working days once documents are verified. LLPs take about 6 days.

What documents do I need to start?

PAN and Aadhaar of all directors, passport photographs, address proof, a registered office proof with owner NOC and a recent utility bill.

Are government fees included in your price?

Our fixed fee covers professional work. Government fees and stamp duty are shown clearly up front and passed on at actuals, with no hidden charges.

Can you handle compliance after registration?

Yes. Our monthly plans cover GST, TDS, ROC filings, income tax and bookkeeping, tracked on your client dashboard.

Do you work with foreign founders?

Yes. We assist foreign directors and NRIs with incorporation, DIN, DSC, FEMA filings and bank account support.

What if my application is rejected by the registrar?

We re-file at no extra professional fee and handle every query raised by the ROC or the tax department until approval.
